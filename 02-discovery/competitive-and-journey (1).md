# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** The Loyal Scroller: A long-term, heavy viewer who has been with the platform for 14 years and opens the app out of habit most evenings.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** She wants to find something she’ll enjoy tonight without spending her evening searching for it.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She scrolls for twenty minutes through 15,000 titles, closes the app without watching anything, and puts on a DVD instead. The platform’s best customer is already finding her evenings outside it.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Her personal DVD collection: a fixed set of films she already owns and trusts, which removes the need to choose from an overwhelming catalogue.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Opens StreamLine out of habit, intending to watch something.
Scrolls through home rows that don’t match her mood or taste.
Tries to narrow it down, but there’s no mood browsing and search only works with exact titles.
Gives up after about twenty minutes and closes the app without watching anything.
Turns to her DVD shelf and picks a familiar film.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment she realises twenty minutes of scrolling have produced nothing. She knows what kind of evening she wants, but the platform can’t understand it, so a small shelf of DVDs feels easier than 15,000 titles.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There’s 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD.” (Priya, UXR-01)
