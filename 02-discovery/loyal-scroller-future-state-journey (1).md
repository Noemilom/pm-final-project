# Future State Journey: The Loyal Scroller

**Persona:** A long-term, heavy viewer who has been with the platform for 14 years and opens the app out of habit most evenings. **Goal:** She wants to find something she'll enjoy tonight without spending her evening searching for it. **Strategy:** Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation over mass-market scale. **Value prop:** Turn StreamLine from a place where you search into a place where you discover, before our best users find their next film, and their next platform, somewhere else.

## The 4 Stages

### 1\. Welcomed, not walled

- **User action:** Opens app \-\> greeted by one curated "tonight's pick," not endless rows.  
- **Internal state:** Curious and calm \-\> feels guided instead of overwhelmed.  
- **Pain point addressed:** 15,000-title wall \-\> replaced by a single, confident starting point.

### 2\. Tells us her mood

- **User action:** Selects "quiet Sunday" \-\> receives a short, hand-picked shelf matching her evening.  
- **Internal state:** Understood \-\> the platform responds to how she actually decides.  
- **Pain point addressed:** No mood browsing or descriptive search \-\> intent finally has an entry point.

### 3\. Chooses with confidence

- **User action:** Reads a curator's note \-\> understands why this film suits her tonight.  
- **Internal state:** Trusting and decisive \-\> chooses in minutes, not twenty.  
- **Pain point addressed:** Repetitive, engagement-driven recommendations \-\> replaced by a trusted human voice.

### 4\. Watches and returns

- **User action:** Plays, pauses, resumes on any device \-\> finishes the film she started.  
- **Internal state:** Satisfied and loyal \-\> StreamLine becomes her default evening again.  
- **Pain point addressed:** Lost watchlists and restart-from-zero \-\> the platform remembers her.

---

## Competitive Advantages over the Manual Workaround (the DVD Shelf)

1. **Fresh discovery, not repetition:** curated picks widen her taste; the DVD shelf loops familiar films.  
2. **Minutes, not a wasted evening:** a guided choice replaces twenty minutes of scrolling then giving up.  
3. **Gets smarter over time:** StreamLine learns her moods across devices; the DVD shelf never changes.
