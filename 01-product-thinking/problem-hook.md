# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Without action, StreamLine keeps its casual users, while failing to attract new subscribers because volume alone is no longer a compelling reason to join. We become stagnant.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Research on what to watch. Discovery happens elsewhere as we offer too much choice and we're not guiding them
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop losing our best users by helping them find what’s worth watching on StreamLine, before they look elsewhere what to watch and find the titles somewhere else
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Spotlight turns StreamLine from a place where you search into a place where you discover, and we need it now, before our best users find their next film, and their next platform, somewhere else.
